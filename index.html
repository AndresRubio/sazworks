06 · FAQ

Questions clients ask

Do you work remotely and across time zones?

Yes. I am in Spain (CET) and have worked remotely with teams across Europe since 2022. Overlap of four hours a day is enough.

Which models and clouds do you use?

Whatever fits your constraints: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or open models such as LLaMA on Groq or your own GPUs. Azure and AWS in production, Kubernetes for both.

Will you sign an NDA or work in our repositories?

Yes to both. I work inside your GitHub or GitLab, follow your review process and leave everything documented.

What is the fastest way to start?

Book a thirty-minute call. If there is a fit, you get a one-page proposal within three working days and we can usually start within two weeks.
